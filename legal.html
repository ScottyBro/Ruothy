No-guarantee statement. Ruothy International does not guarantee admission, employment, sponsorship, assessment results, visas or placement. We provide credible pathways, preparation and coordination. Final decisions always rest with institutions, employers, testing bodies and relevant authorities.

Privacy notice

This notice explains how Ruothy International (Private) Limited (“Ruothy”, “we”) collects, uses and protects your personal information, and the rights you have. We aim to handle your data lawfully, fairly and transparently, in line with applicable data-protection law including Zimbabwe’s Cyber and Data Protection Act.

What we collect

  • Enquiry details you give us: name, phone/WhatsApp, email, country, your goal and current stage, and anything you write in a message.
  • Partner enquiry details: your name, organisation, role, email, country and partnership interest.
  • Technical data: limited, privacy-respecting analytics about how the site is used (see Cookies & analytics).

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To respond to your enquiry and route it to the right service or partner — on the basis of your consent and to take steps you have requested.
  • To operate, secure and improve the website — on the basis of our legitimate interests, balanced against your rights.
  • To meet legal, safeguarding and record-keeping obligations where they apply.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry data only as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected, after which it is deleted or anonymised. Retention periods are set in our internal governance schedule.

Who we share it with

We share information with relevant institutions, employers or specialist partners only where needed to support the request you have made, and under appropriate confidentiality terms. We use trusted service providers (for example email and hosting) who process data on our instructions. We never sell your data, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

International transfers

Because opportunities are international, your information may be shared with organisations outside Zimbabwe. Where it is, we take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

Security & data minimisation

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data. Critically, we never request passports, certificates, bank statements, medical information or full candidate files through general website forms. Where such documents are genuinely needed, we use a secured, purpose-specific process only after need, authority and consent are established.

Children

Our services are aimed at adults and older learners. Where we work with minors, we do so only with appropriate consent and safeguards.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, update or delete your information, to restrict or object to certain uses, or to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise a right, contact study.info@ruothy.com. You also have the right to complain to the relevant data-protection authority.

Cookies & analytics

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We keep our use of them to a minimum:

  • Essential — needed for the site to function and to remember your cookie choice. These are always on and store no personal identifiers.
  • Analytics (optional) — privacy-respecting measurement of how the site is used, so we can improve it. We exclude form content and sensitive identifiers, and we do not build advertising profiles. These run only if you accept.

We don’t use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and we don’t add third-party trackers, widgets or AI services without a privacy, security and data-flow review. You can change your mind at any time.

Terms of use

By using this website you agree to use it lawfully and not to misuse its content, forms or contact channels, attempt to gain unauthorised access, or submit false or others’ personal information without permission.

Information is provided for general guidance and may change without notice. Programme and opportunity details carry status and review dates; expired opportunities are archived and must not be treated as active. All content, branding and design on this site belong to Ruothy International or its licensors and may not be copied or reused without permission. To the extent permitted by law, we are not liable for indirect or consequential loss arising from use of the site or reliance on general information.

Website & opportunity-listing disclaimer

Content is provided in good faith for information only and does not constitute regulated immigration, legal, financial or admissions advice. Regulated matters are handled by, or handed off to, appropriately authorised and licensed specialists.

Every opportunity listing aims to show the responsible organisation, eligibility source, approved fees, deadline and last-reviewed date. Automated recommendations (for example from the “Explore” questionnaire) are informational guidance to help you start — they are not eligibility or admission decisions. Institutions, employers, testing bodies and authorities make all final decisions.

Safeguarding

We are committed to ethical, safe practice for the individuals, families and cohorts we work with. In practice this means we explain responsibilities, limitations and data use before asking for anything sensitive; we hand off regulated matters to authorised specialists; we never pressure you or promise outcomes; and we maintain clear escalation routes.

If you ever feel misled, pressured or unsafe in any interaction connected to Ruothy — including by anyone claiming to represent us — please tell us immediately using the contacts below so we can look into it.

To raise a complaint, report a concern, or make a data request (access, correction or deletion), contact us with a short description and any reference details. We will acknowledge your request, route it to our governance queue, and respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Partner-logo governance

We display partner names, logos, links and descriptions only where approved by the partner, and we represent partner authority and responsibilities accurately. Test owners and their marks are named only where written authorisation exists. If you believe your organisation’s mark is shown without approval, contact us and we will act promptly.

Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use this site, and we target WCAG 2.2 AA. That includes semantic structure, keyboard navigation, visible focus, labelled forms, meaningful alt text, sufficient colour contrast, respect for reduced-motion preferences, and performance on common devices and slower networks.

Accessibility is an ongoing effort and some areas may still fall short. If you experience a barrier or need information in another format, contact study.info@ruothy.com and we will help and put it right where we can.
