Choosing a credible study destination

Choosing where to study is one of the biggest decisions you’ll make. Use this checklist to compare options with a clear head — before you commit time or money.

1. Check accreditation and recognition

An institution can look impressive and still not be recognised where it matters. Confirm the school is accredited by the relevant national body, and that the specific qualification is recognised by employers or professional bodies in the countries you care about.

  • Find the accreditation on an official register, not just the institution’s own website.
  • Check that your programme — not only the institution — is recognised.
  • Ask how graduates of your programme have progressed.

2. Understand the real cost

Tuition is only part of the picture. Build a realistic budget that includes living costs, accommodation, insurance, travel and any application or assessment fees.

  • Ask for a full, written cost breakdown.
  • Be cautious of any “scholarship” that requires large upfront payments.
  • Confirm what is and isn’t included in quoted fees.

3. Look at outcomes and support

Credible institutions are open about graduate outcomes and the support they offer international students — orientation, academic help, wellbeing and careers services.

4. Questions to ask before you commit

  • Who makes the final admission decision, and what are the exact requirements?
  • What are the deadlines, and what happens if plans change?
  • What support exists for international students on arrival?
