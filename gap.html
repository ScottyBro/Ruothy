GAP — the Global Access Program

Preparation that builds real readiness. GAP develops the language, employability, vocational, digital-assessment and pre-departure skills that make global pathways achievable — delivered through structured cohorts.

Register your interest

What GAP builds

Readiness across five areas

Language

Communication and academic English readiness for study and work environments.

Employability

CVs, interviews, workplace conduct and the soft skills employers value.

Vocational

Practical, sector-relevant skills mapped to real opportunity areas.

Digital assessment

Confidence with online testing formats and digital readiness.

Pre-departure

Practical preparation for a responsible, well-informed transition.

Cohorts & academies

GAP runs as structured cohorts and academies beneath one clearly branded Ruothy programme.
