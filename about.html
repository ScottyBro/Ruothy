Who we are

A trusted pathway into global opportunity

Ruothy International is a global-opportunity platform based in Harare, Zimbabwe. We coordinate an individual’s journey — from aspiration and discovery through preparation, assessment and responsible transition — working through defined responsibilities and trusted partners.

Our story

Built to connect, prepare and do it responsibly

Too often, global opportunity is fragmented and over-promised. Ruothy exists to change that — bringing education, skills, assessment and mobility into one coordinated journey, so people move forward well-prepared and clearly informed.

We’re proudly African and internationally connected, giving individuals confidence that we understand the whole journey, and giving institutions and employers confidence that we work through preparation, evidence and accountable partnerships.

Our values

Experience principles we hold to

Human before service catalogue

We lead with people, ambitions and credible transformation — not lists of destinations or vacancies.

Journey before transaction

We show the sequence from discovery to preparation and responsible transition.

Trust before conversion

We explain responsibilities, limitations and data use before asking for anything sensitive.

Clarity before volume

Concise copy and clear next steps, instead of dense, overwhelming pages.

Mobile before desktop

Designed first for phones, WhatsApp hand-off and constrained bandwidth.

Opportunity without overpromising

We never imply guaranteed admission, employment, sponsorship, results, visas or placement.
